Practical Notes Datos Prácticos

Money

Currency is ARS (Argentine peso). Inflation is high; rates change weekly.

Use a foreign Visa/Mastercard at restaurants and hotels — they're now charged at the official rate, which is close to the "blue" street rate. Best deal without effort.

For cash, bring crisp USD bills (twenties or hundreds, no folds or marks) and exchange via Western Union for the best rate. Skip the airport ATMs.

Language

Spanish, but porteño Spanish is distinctive:

· vos instead of tú (you)
· "ll" and "y" pronounced "sh" (calle = "ka-shay")
· "che" used like "hey"
· Italian-inflected rhythm and gestures

English is common in Recoleta, Palermo, and at the hotel. Spotty elsewhere — download Google Translate offline.

Weather

Late June is mid-winter. Expect 45–60°F (7–15°C) with cool nights and the occasional rainy day.

Pack: warm layers, waterproof shell, walking shoes, gloves and beanie for Minecraft night, a scarf for variability.

Sunset around 5:50 pm.

Getting Around

Uber is the default — cheap, plentiful, safer than street cabs. Cabify is the local equivalent.

Walk within Recoleta and to Palermo Soho. From the hotel: Recoleta Cemetery (7 min), El Ateneo (10 min), Galería Bond Street (10 min).

Subte (subway) is fine in daylight, crowded at rush hour, sketchier after 10 pm. Buy a SUBE card at any station (~ARS 1,000 + load). Closest stations to hotel: Callao (Line D) or Pueyrredón (Line D).

Subway-friendly trips on this itinerary (all daylight):
· San Telmo / Plaza de Mayo → Line D, then transfer
· Templo Libertad area → Line D Tribunales
· AMIA → Line B Pasteur-AMIA
· Galería Bond Street → walk or Line D Callao

Always Uber: La Boca (no good subway), Ciudad Universitaria (night, no good transit), EZE airport, Reserva Ecológica (poor connections), anything after dark with bags or valuables.

Eating Rhythms

Locals eat dinner at 9–10 pm. Restaurants don't fill until then; arriving at 7 pm feels eerie.

Lunch is 1–3 pm. Many places close 4–8 pm.

Tipping ~10% in cash is appreciated. Most places add a small cubierto (cover/bread) charge.

Safety

Recoleta and Palermo are very safe day and night. Use the same big-city awareness as anywhere — bag on the inside of your shoulder, phone not displayed, no flashy jewelry.

The most common issue is petty theft / phone snatching on busy streets like Florida. Park Hyatt has a doorman and arrivals are safe.
