Jewish history ✡walkMetro L3
📍 Start: Plaza Mayor 🔗 VoiceMap tour
The popular "Lavapiés was the Jewish quarter" story is mostly legend; conversos likely lived there after 1492. Madrid's real medieval Jewish quarters were near the Teatro Real and Calle del Arenal, and later by the Plaza de la Armería, where the Almudena Cathedral stands. Wednesday's palace visit covers those streets.
~12:15 – 1:30 pm · lunch · TOP PRIORITY
★ Mercado de San Miguel

A 1916 iron-and-glass market, now a gourmet food hall a few steps from Plaza Mayor. Take a slow lap, then graze for lunch. Mya Valdalos is the cheese stall: a good first stop for Spanish cheeses.

Safest picks: oysters, jamón ibérico, cheese, olives, vermouth, fresh juice. Skip anything on bread, croquetas, and fried items, since nothing here is dedicated GF.

👑 Palacio Real + Royal Kitchens

Tickets are bought. Go in after the changing of the guard, around 11:15, and do the self-guided tour of the state rooms. That leaves about an hour before the Royal Kitchens tour at 12:15. Ask at the entrance where the kitchens tour meets.

Getting Around & Eating Safely

🚇 Madrid public transit

Nearest Metro stations to the hotel: Santo Domingo (L2), Callao (L3/L5), Ópera (L2/L5/R). Get a reusable Tarjeta Multi at any machine and load 10-trip tickets. A tourist pass (Abono Turístico) is the alternative. Toledo trains leave from Atocha, which is also the Metro stop for the Retiro and Botánico. Cercanías stops at Sol.

🚕 Airport

Taxi fare is fixed at €33 from any terminal to anywhere inside the M-30, around the clock, with no luggage or holiday supplements (Tariff 4, 2026). A taxi takes 4 passengers. Lufthansa flights use T2.

🌾 Gluten-free phrases

  • Soy celíaco/a. I have celiac disease.
  • ¿Es sin gluten? ¿Hay contaminación cruzada? Is it gluten-free? Any cross-contamination?
  • ¿Lo fríen en aceite aparte? Is it fried in separate oil?
  • German: Ich habe Zöliakie. Ist das glutenfrei?
