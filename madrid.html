★ Mercado de San Miguel

A 1916 iron-and-glass market, now a gourmet food hall a few steps from Plaza Mayor. Take a slow lap, then graze for lunch. Mya Valdalos is the cheese stall: a good first stop for Spanish cheeses.

Safest picks: oysters, jamón ibérico, cheese, olives, vermouth, fresh juice. Skip anything on bread, croquetas, and fried items, since nothing here is dedicated GF.

💂 Changing of the Guard: Puerta del Príncipe
Every Wednesday and Saturday from 11:00 to 14:00, guards are relieved every half hour at the Puerta del Príncipe. It's free, but it can be called off in bad weather. Watch the 11:00 relief, then go into the palace. The big monthly Relevo Solemne is the first Wednesday (Dec 2), so we just miss it.

👑 Palacio Real + Royal Kitchens

Tickets are bought. Go in after the changing of the guard, around 11:15, and do the self-guided tour of the state rooms. That leaves about an hour before the Royal Kitchens tour at 12:15. Ask at the entrance where the kitchens tour meets.

This is the official residence of the Spanish royal family, used for state ceremonies (the King and Queen live at the Zarzuela). Since March 2026 the visit also includes the reliquary rooms of the Royal Chapel. Afterwards, step across to the Almudena Cathedral, where Felipe and Letizia married in 2004.

✡ Guided Jewish history tour of Toledo (2.5 hours)

🖼 Museo Reina Sofía

Spain's national museum of modern art, home to Picasso's Guernica, plus Dalí and Miró. Entry is currently through the Sabatini Building on Plaza de Juan Goytisolo, because the Nouvel entrance is closed. From the hotel, about 25 minutes by Metro (Line 1 from Sol to Estación del Arte) or 10 minutes by taxi.

Tickets: buy 2 adult tickets online. Under-18s get in free, but their free tickets are issued only at the ticket desk on showing an official ID, so bring the kids' passports. Under-14s must be with an adult.

🌳 Real Jardín Botánico + El Retiro

Botánico first, since it closes earlier (about 18:00 in November; entry about €4–6). It's Carlos III's 1781 garden on Plaza de Murillo next to the Prado, with greenhouses and the bonsai collection. Then cross into El Retiro: the boating lake and Alfonso XII monument, the Palacio de Cristal (check it's open), and the Rosaleda.

The Botánico is about 10 minutes' walk from the museum. Walk north through the Retiro and leave by the Puerta de Alcalá, then take Metro Line 2 from Retiro to Noviciado for Conde Duque, about 20 minutes in all.

🎄 German Christmas market: choose one
A · Frankfurt: Römerberg + Paulsplatz + Mainkai recommended
One of Germany's oldest and largest Christmas markets, dating to 1393. Open Nov 23 – Dec 22, Mon–Sat 10:00–21:00. About 15 minutes on S8/S9 from Gateway Gardens to Hauptwache. Walk Hauptwache → Römerberg (giant tree, half-timbered square, carousel) → down to the Main. Easiest option on a travel day.
📍 Römerberg
B · Mainz: historic market at the cathedral
Opens Thu Nov 26, 2026, so it's in its first weekend. The market sits at the foot of the Romanesque cathedral, with 110+ wooden stalls, a Christmas pyramid, and Mainz-wine Glühwein. S8 runs directly from Gateway Gardens to Mainz in about half an hour.
📍 Mainz Dom
C · Cologne: market at the Dom (biggest wow, most effort)
Open Nov 16 – Dec 23, Thu–Sat 11:00–22:00, on Roncalliplatz under the cathedral, 2 minutes from Köln Hbf. ICE from Frankfurt Flughafen Fernbahnhof takes about 50–55 minutes. It's a late night: back around 11pm before an early-afternoon flight.
📍 Roncalliplatz
🌾 GF at the market: Glühwein, Kinderpunsch, roasted chestnuts, and candied almonds are usually naturally gluten-free. Lebkuchen, Stollen, crêpes, and Reibekuchen (potato pancakes, usually made with flour) are not. For Bratwurst, ask "glutenfrei?" and get it without the roll. Getting Around & Eating Safely

🚇 Madrid public transit

Nearest Metro stations to the hotel: Santo Domingo (L2), Callao (L3/L5), Ópera (L2/L5/R). Get a reusable Tarjeta Multi at any machine and load 10-trip tickets. A tourist pass (Abono Turístico) is the alternative. Toledo trains leave from Atocha, which is also the Metro stop for the Retiro and Botánico. Cercanías stops at Sol.

🚕 Airport

Taxi fare is fixed at €33 from any terminal to anywhere inside the M-30, around the clock, with no luggage or holiday supplements (Tariff 4, 2026). A taxi takes 4 passengers. Lufthansa flights use T2.

🌾 Gluten-free phrases

  • Soy celíaco/a. I have celiac disease.
  • ¿Es sin gluten? ¿Hay contaminación cruzada? Is it gluten-free? Any cross-contamination?
  • ¿Lo fríen en aceite aparte? Is it fried in separate oil?
  • German: Ich habe Zöliakie. Ist das glutenfrei?

🛒 Supermarket backup

Mercadona's house brand (Hacendado) tests for gluten and labels GF items sin gluten. It's good for room snacks and plane food.

🎄 Christmas in Madrid

The lights switched on Sat Nov 21 and stay up through Jan 6. Best streets: Gran Vía, Puerta del Sol, Plaza Mayor, Calle Preciados, Serrano. The Plaza Mayor Christmas market opens Fri Nov 27 and runs to Dec 31.
